## Method

Forced `page.route` POST response 404 / 409 / 500 in three shapes: server-flash (the app's OOB `#flash-region` partial), plain-body (`nope`, client builds the flash from the status), error-toast (empty body plus `HX-Trigger: {"toast":{"variant":"error",...}}`). Counted visible `.rd-toast`, `dialog[open] .rd-flash`, `#flash-region .rd-flash`, `.rd-field__error`/`.rd-dialog__errors`. Widths 1440, 1024, 375. 147 rows per app.
