Step 3b:

1. Swap targets: every `[hx-target]` selector on the four routes matches exactly one element at all three
   viewports (`badSwapTargets` empty).
2. Failing saves: the New account form on `/accounts` with forced 404, 409 and 500 responses shows a visible
   flash each time ("That item was not found...", "That item changed in the meantime...", "Something went
   wrong on our side..."); no silent failure. The touched templates add no forms.
3. No arithmetic in templates: the template diff adds only `role`, `aria-*`, `data-testid` attributes and
   removes one `aria-hidden`. The only expression in an added attribute is `[spent, 100]|min`, the clamp the
   macro already used for the bar width. No `float`, `/ 100`, `* 100`, new `set`, or literals added.
4. Money formatting: `/accounts` shows the EUR account as `100.00` and the JPY account whole (`12,345`); the
   credit card shows `Available 3,765.44`.
5. Rendered number equals the service value: `/today` hero per-day `39.44` and bar `aria-valuenow` 24.0 on the
   scratch data; the exact comparison against `budget_service`/`view_models` is
   `tests/integration/web/test_budget_view_values.py` (hero, plan period bar, row cells, remainder bar), green.
6. IDs and listeners: no duplicate ids after opening the New account slide-over (HTMX swap); no new
   `HX-Trigger` events or listeners were introduced.
7. Scratch data only: confirmed.
