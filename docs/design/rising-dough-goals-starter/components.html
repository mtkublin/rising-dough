Loaf shapes

.rd-loaf[data-shape][data-color]

Three cell tones per loaf: body --cat; crust color-mix(--cat 70%, #0d0d0b); score color-mix(--cat 45%, #0d0d0b). Shape is fixed per goal: crc32("rd:" + goal id) % 4, so Vacation is the same loaf everywhere. A loaf stays once baked, even if the goal later drops below target or is archived.
